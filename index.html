How to use

  1. Add photos from your gallery, or shoot with Camera. You can add several at once.
  2. Auto all does the whole job in one tap: finds the page edges, straightens the tilt, and cleans the result. Use it unless you want to set the crop and the look yourself.
  3. Auto crop only finds the page edges and the tilt, leaving the look as it is. If it misses the page, drag the dots on the preview to set the crop yourself — a dot on each corner, and one on the middle of each side to move that whole side at once. The picture zooms in on the dot as you take hold of it, so you can land it on the printed edge — or rotate with Left / Right and try again, or set Fine angle by hand. Undo all puts the page back to the photo you imported — crop, straighten, rotation and look.
  4. Auto clean only sets the look, leaving the crop as it is. Pick one yourself instead if you prefer. For OCR of typed text, B/W gives the crispest result. Shadow removal, under Custom, lifts a shadow off the paper in every mode. If the text is blurry, Sharpen in the same row taps up through three steps — a fourth tap puts back what the look asked for — and it stacks on top of whichever look you picked.
  5. The preview always shows the finished page, exactly as it will be exported. Pinch it to zoom in and check that small print is legible, and drag with two fingers to move around; it springs back to fit when you change pages.
  6. Done bakes the result into the page. Export to a multi-page PDF or to individual images.

Tip: fill the frame with the document, avoid hard shadows across the page, and keep the camera roughly parallel to the paper.
